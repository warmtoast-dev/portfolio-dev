Project: Costco Slop

2026-09-26

Why

  • I kept seeing YouTube videos explaining Costco's business model and got curious to know how many such videos existed.
  • Apparently, quite a lot.
  • I'm still learning to build things. This was an excuse to make something and ship it.
  • Mostly writing this so future me remembers what I did.

What I made

  • A collection of Costco videos.
  • Sort by latest, oldest and most viewed.
  • Filter by year and creator.
  • Random video button.
  • An unnecessary Costco quiz.
  • Made to look like YouTube in the 2010s.
  • No ads, accounts, tracking or monetisation.

How I made it

  • Almost all the code was written with ChatGPT and Claude.
  • I provided the directions and kept asking them to make changes. I wouldn't have been able to ship it without them.

The numbers

  • First attempt: ~40 videos.
  • Changed how I searched YouTube.
  • manually searched 2026 videos and found ~400 videos before I hit the daily limit.
  • Version 1: 432 videos by 384 creators in 13 years with ~40 million combined views.
  • There are obviously way more than these.
  • I've hit my daily YouTube API quota, so this is partial data.
  • There are Shorts and some bad results. I'll keep adding and cleaning it up.
